# Publication Scope

This repository presents selected scientific findings and limitations from Explorations 007–025.

The complete historical reference is retained separately. The figures presented here come from simulations described in the research synthesis; the findings do not establish the existence of subjective consciousness.

This repository does not contain the complete programs or raw data needed for full reproduction. The SHA-256 fingerprint of the historical reference is provided in the README.
